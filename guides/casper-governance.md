# Casper governance: validator votes CVV001 to CVV010, the Casper Association and its published accounts

Verified against casper-node v2.2.2 on 2026-09-23.

Since October 2024, mainnet validators have voted on chain on numbered proposals, CVV001 to CVV010 as of 2026-09-23, covering testnet reward programs, the Casper Association's delegation policy and the network's fee and issuance rules. Each proposal is posted on the [Casper forum](https://forum.casper.network/), validators vote by sending a voting token to one of three ballot accounts, and the Casper Association, the Swiss association that issues CSPR ([casper.network: Sustainability & MiCA Transparency](https://www.casper.network/sustainability-mica-transparency), read 2026-09-23), announces some of the outcomes on casper.network. For each vote, this guide gives what was proposed (the forum text), what was decided (the on-chain tally and what Casper published) and when it took effect (the mainnet upgrade and the casper-node configuration). It then covers what the Casper Association is, how its delegation program picks validators, and which of its accounts have been published. It states no price, prediction, investment advice or tax advice.

Six kinds of source are used here, and they do not all stay the same. **Forum topics** were read on 2026-09-23 through the forum's `/raw/<id>` endpoint, one request per topic. A topic can be edited later, and the time in a raw text's header is its last edit, not when it was posted: CVV002's header reads 2025-09-17 for a topic created on 2024-12-03 ([topic](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv002-casper-association-delegation-policy/1220), read 2026-09-23). **casper.network posts** were read on 2026-09-23. An article page does not show its own date, so the dates given here are the ones the site's [news index](https://www.casper.network/news) showed that day. **Casper's Telegram announcement channel**, [t.me/casperofficialann](https://t.me/casperofficialann), describes itself as the "Official Announcement Channel of the Casper Network overseen by the Casper Association", and casper-node's README links it ([README.md#L34](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/README.md#L34)). Its posts from September 2024 to September 2026 were read on 2026-09-23 through the channel's public web preview, and each post's date is the one the channel shows. **Chain state** was read from a public mainnet node, `https://node.mainnet.casper.network/rpc`, on 2026-09-23. State at a past block does not change. **casper-node** is cited at the v2.2.2 tag, together with the mainnet configuration files that `genesis.casper.network` serves. The **MiCA white paper** and the **validator-selection-tool** repository are cited at the file and commit given. This repository's weekly check follows casper-node releases only. It does not follow the forum, casper.network, the Telegram channel, the chain or the white paper, so a new vote (CVV011 or later) will not mark this guide as stale.

A plan, schedule or commitment quoted below is what its source said on the date given. It is not a statement that the thing happened.

## How a Casper governance vote works

- **From idea to vote.** Each final proposal links an earlier discussion topic ("The discussion topic" in CVV001 to CVV003, "The feedback collection & discussion topic" from CVV004 on) and gives its own address as "The final proposal topic". It lists three voting options and gives a voting window by block height, from CVV003 on, or by deadline for CVV001 and CVV002 ([CVV002](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv002-casper-association-delegation-policy/1220), [CVV005](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv005-proposal-enable-burning-mechanism/1538), read 2026-09-23).

- **Who votes.** Only mainnet validators vote. CVV003 to CVV010 say: "A voting token, with a total supply of 100.00, for the proposal will be created, and distributed to Mainnet validators based on their network weights as of era" a named era ([CVV005](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv005-proposal-enable-burning-mechanism/1538), read 2026-09-23). CVV001 says the token "has been created" as of era 15617, and CVV002 uses weights "at the end of era 16057" ([CVV001](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv001-incentivized-testnet-proposal-casper-network-testnet-rewards-program-nov-11-dec-31-2024/778), [CVV002](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv002-casper-association-delegation-policy/1220), read 2026-09-23). From CVV004 on, validators "send all (recommended) voting tokens to one of the corresponding addresses" ([CVV005](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv005-proposal-enable-burning-mechanism/1538)); CVV001 to CVV003 say "send all (recommended) or some of the voting tokens" ([CVV002](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv002-casper-association-delegation-policy/1220)), both read 2026-09-23. Each token is a CEP-18 contract named "Casper Validator Voting Token" followed by the vote's number, with symbol `CVVT001` to `CVVT010`, 2 decimals and a total supply of 100.00 (chain state read 2026-09-23). The instructions for vote 008 put it this way: "1.00 CVVT008 = 100 MOTES" ([casper.network, 23 Feb 2026](https://www.casper.network/news/governance-vote-008-voting-instructions-for-validators), read 2026-09-23).

- **The rule that decides.** Every proposal says: "Quorum ratio: +50% network weight (>50.00 tokens, including the abstains)" and "Decision threshold: Simple majority (+50% of participating weight, excluding the abstains)" ([CVV008](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv008-proposal-securing-validator-economics-and-network-sustainability/1567), read 2026-09-23). Casper's explanation, from December 2024: "only For and Against votes are counted to determine the outcome, excluding abstentions. A simple majority of these adjusted votes is required for a proposal to pass, provided that at least 50% of the network's weight participates in the vote" ([casper.network, 19 Dec 2024](https://www.casper.network/news/achieving-decentralization-michael-steuer), read 2026-09-23).

- **The three ballot accounts, the same for every vote so far.** For `011de3a86cd71d98a83bddf57384e1a0c3b4ea5be696fcbd6fa9a80b3cdcf396de`, Against `0118c145c88386f6cf0dd12c30702742013ab23260253e8748f620dd31c27ccadb`, Abstain `015dc16c2072eaf747f559385ee0ac277fbf91ab87b536da8c5bb6177940e833ac` ([CVV001](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv001-incentivized-testnet-proposal-casper-network-testnet-rewards-program-nov-11-dec-31-2024/778) through [CVV010](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv010-incentivized-testnet-proposal-casper-network-testnet-rewards-program-july-01-dec-31-2026/1575), read 2026-09-23). The command-line instructions send to their account hashes: `account-hash-dee89722314bc49c405097734b63928293da164343d63e59e92d0000ba2d7870` (For), `account-hash-881228ea4bdf3712c7883d6f08460541847a4543b56db001be7559e989a6b997` (Against) and `account-hash-1e1ab215aee835e1fc2bfc2fe80c373bb57d9e5b4f019c66654367aad4b177f0` (Abstain) ([casper.network, 23 Feb 2026](https://www.casper.network/news/governance-vote-008-voting-instructions-for-validators), read 2026-09-23). These accounts only collect ballots. No source calls them treasury accounts.

- **Delegators.** Delegators hold no voting token. The MiCA white paper says: "In order to influence the network's governance, a tokenholder must stake its token" ([white paper, p. 22](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/668fef77d8ac075ed5e3f57a/68da95d664a37c9e855f816d_Whitepaper%20V3%20Submission.pdf#page=22), read 2026-09-23). In 2024 the Association described the plan as validators voting "on behalf of their delegators", with delegators able to "redelegate their stake to another validator if they disagree with their validator's vote" ([casper.network, 9 Oct 2024](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-association-announces-board-transition-and-new-community-centered-vision), read 2026-09-23).

- **Where results are shown.** casper.network links results to [cspr.vote/vote-results](https://cspr.vote/vote-results) ([casper.network, 23 Mar 2026](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-2-2-0-goes-live-on-mainnet), read 2026-09-23). Earlier posts linked `eraguardian.com/mainnet/vote-results`, which casper.network called "a community-built governance dashboard" ([casper.network, 2 Oct 2025](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-community-x-space-recap-september-30-2025)). On 2026-09-23 that page said the voting functionality "has been migrated to a dedicated platform" and linked to cspr.vote. cspr.vote describes itself as "Transparent On-Chain Voting for Casper Network" and lists legacy result pages for votes 001 to 010 in its [sitemap](https://cspr.vote/sitemap.xml). Its result pages fill in their figures in the browser: the HTML they served on 2026-09-23 reads "For 0 Against 0 Abstain 0". Its robots.txt disallows `/api/`, so this guide did not read figures from cspr.vote and counted the votes on chain instead (next section).

- **The forum's "Stage" line and tags are not a record of the outcome.** See Corrections, item 1.

## CVV002 and CVV009: the Casper Association delegation policy

- **What CVV002 set (voted 4 to 8 Dec 2024).** Its goal is "A systematic and transparent delegation process, to enhance network decentralization and incentivise optimal validator performance". Each "Delegation Round is 90 days", and "The number of validators selected in each Delegation Round is 30". To be eligible, and to keep a delegation, a validator needs "Minimum of 3 months as an active validator" and a "Performance score of 98% or higher for the past 30 days (as shown on cspr.live)". It must be "identified with the Casper Account Information Standard", be a member of "the 'Validator Guild' Telegram group", have "'Validator' status on the official Casper forum", give the Association direct contact information, take part in on-chain governance votes, and charge a "Commission rate between 1% and 10% (validators with 0% commission are ineligible)". The Association allocates "60% of the delegation stake to the 10 lowest-weighted eligible validators" and 40% to the other eligible validators. "Top 10 validators by stake and validators with over 1,200 delegators will be excluded". A validator chosen for one round "will be excluded from the next round" ([CVV002](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv002-casper-association-delegation-policy/1220), read 2026-09-23).

- **What CVV009 changed (voted 30 Mar to 1 Apr 2026).** It replaces the 60/40 split with a target. With S10 the total stake of the validator ranked 10th, "T = 0.9 × S10". Starting from the lowest-ranked eligible validator below the top 10, the Association delegates "only up to the amount required to reach Target Stake (T)", first to one node per entity (its lowest-ranked node), then with the entity filter lifted. Anything left over is split "equally per validator" among validators that meet every criterion except the top-10 exclusion. From "July 01" every eligible mainnet validator must "Operate a Testnet validator node for each Mainnet validator node", paired "via Casper Account Info Contract on Testnet", with "≥ 99% monthly performance or daily performance". The proposal removes "Any requirement relying on manual or off-chain verification", naming "Forum participation checks" and "Manual identity or activity validation by the Association". It recommends undelegating from higher-ranked validators first, without changing the ranking. It adds that "The Association may, on an exceptional basis, exclude or suspend validators from consideration where required to address legal, regulatory, or contractual risk" ([CVV009](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv009-update-to-the-association-delegation-policy/1570), read 2026-09-23). CVV009 does not list which CVV002 criteria count as off-chain, and it does not restate the round length or the 30-validator count.

- **Its outcome.** On chain, CVV009 got 67.55 For, 0 Against and 17.84 Abstain (tally above). Casper's announcement channel reported on 1 Apr 2026 that "CVV009 has officially passed!", with "85.39% Participation" and "100% In Favor" ([t.me/casperofficialann/1608](https://t.me/casperofficialann/1608), read 2026-09-23). No casper.network post announces it.

- **How to be considered.** No application form has been published. CVV009 says "Mainnet operators can join Testnet any time they like. There is no need to apply or participate in lotteries etc." ([CVV009](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv009-update-to-the-association-delegation-policy/1570), read 2026-09-23). The Account Information Standard is a JSON file on the owner's domain, linked on chain to the account ([casper-account-info-standard README](https://github.com/make-software/casper-account-info-standard/blob/d614ae82178a9629a017e9a628152fdd49a8715b/README.md#L83-L90)). Its mainnet contract is `fb8e0215c040691e9bbe945dd22a00989b532b9c2521582538edb95b61156698`, which the contract's README lists with "Casper Association" as owner ([casper-account-info-contract README](https://github.com/make-software/casper-account-info-contract/blob/8a6def40c5a238ffe9a4942f25f0a64bfd2ef9c9/README.md#L66-L73)).

- **Where the treasury's stake goes.** After the CVV008 issuance, Casper said the remaining allocation "will be staked on the network in accordance with the validator-approved treasury delegation policies under proposal #002" ([casper.network, 23 Mar 2026](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-2-2-0-goes-live-on-mainnet), read 2026-09-23).

### The validator-selection-tool

- **What it is.** [casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool) is "a Python script designed to assist in the transparent and fair selection of validators for the Casper Association's staking program". It reads CSPR.cloud with the user's own API key and writes two CSV files, all validators and "the filtered list of eligible validators for delegation" ([README.md#L3](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/README.md#L3), [#L44-L48](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/README.md#L44-L48)). It has no releases. Its newest commit on 2026-09-23 was [f8775bb](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/commit/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440), "Add vote CVV010", of 2026-08-20.

- **What its filter checks at f8775bb.** A validator passes if it had a performance score above 0 in each of the eras 360, 720 and 1080 before the last era, has "average_performance" of at least 98.0, has an active account-info record, charges a fee from 1 to 10, has fewer than 1,200 delegators, and took part in at least half of the listed votes ([fetch_validators.py#L111-L124](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/fetch_validators.py#L111-L124), [#L235-L238](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/fetch_validators.py#L235-L238)). Participation is read from CSPR.cloud as a token action by the validator's account on that vote's token inside the vote's block range ([#L79-L108](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/fetch_validators.py#L79-L108)). A validator whose `network_share` is below 0.01 counts as having voted in every round, "because they do not have voting tokens due to low weight" ([#L243-L248](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/fetch_validators.py#L243-L248)). The top-10 condition is commented out ([#L119](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/fetch_validators.py#L119)).

- **What it does not do at f8775bb.** It computes no allocation, neither CVV002's 60/40 split nor CVV009's target T. It does not check CVV009's paired testnet node. It does not check CVV002's Telegram-group, forum-status or contact criteria, its one-node-per-validator rule or its exclusion of the previous round's validators ([fetch_validators.py#L111-L124](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/fetch_validators.py#L111-L124)). The README describes it as aligned with the CVV002 policy only ([README.md#L75-L77](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/README.md#L75-L77)). How the Association turns the candidate list into amounts is not in the repository.

- **An older page with different criteria.** casper.network's "Casper Association: Staking Update", which the news index dates 19 Aug 2022, gives a "Target maximum commission rate (rake): 5%", a "Typical range of staked CSPR on a validator: Minimum 1 000 000 CSPR; Maximum 100 000 000 CSPR" and "Typical period of delegation is between 90 and 120 days" ([casper.network: Staking Update](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-association-staking-update), read 2026-09-23). The voted rules above replace it; do not quote it as the current policy.

## Corrections: Casper governance statements that are out of date or disagree (checked 2026-09-23)

1. **Forum Stage lines and tags do not record outcomes.** The "Stage" line in the raw text reads "Accepted" for CVV001 and CVV002, "Discussion" for CVV008, "Pre-Vote" for CVV009 and "Pre-voting" for the other six, even though all ten passed on chain. The topic tags read `accepted` for CVV001, 002, 006 and 008, `accepted` and `implemented` for CVV003 and 004, and `pre-voting` for CVV005, 007, 009 and 010. Sources: the ten topics' raw texts and their `/t/<id>.json`, read 2026-09-23 (for example [CVV005](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv005-proposal-enable-burning-mechanism/1538), which passed and is in force, still says "Pre-voting").

2. **CVV001 did not pass "in mid-November".** casper.network wrote that "the first On-Chain Vote was passed in mid-November" ([casper.network, 19 Dec 2024](https://www.casper.network/news/achieving-decentralization-michael-steuer), read 2026-09-23). The proposal's deadline was "23:59 UTC, October 30, 2024" ([CVV001](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv001-incentivized-testnet-proposal-casper-network-testnet-rewards-program-nov-11-dec-31-2024/778)), and the window the selection tool records closed at block 3,808,400, timestamped 2024-10-30 23:24 UTC (chain, read 2026-09-23).

3. **Vote counts in recaps do not match the numbered votes.** A recap of 30 Jan 2025 lists "three on-chain votes already completed" as "Onchain Voting System for Validators, Testnet Incentive Program, and Casper Association's Delegation Policy" ([casper.network](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-x-space-recap-january-28-2025)). By then the numbered votes were CVV001 (testnet), CVV002 (delegation) and CVV003 (testnet). A recap of 27 Mar 2025 mentions a further vote "to move Casper 2.0 onto the testnet" ([casper.network](https://www.casper.network/news/x-spaces-recap-march-2025)). The year-end recap of 19 Dec 2025 counts "six on-chain governance votes" in 2025 ([casper.network](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-end-of-year-x-space-recap-2025-in-review-new-dex-announced-live-and-the-path-forward)). Only four numbered votes, CVV003 to CVV006, had windows in 2025. All pages were read 2026-09-23. No vote titled "Onchain Voting System for Validators", and no vote on moving Casper 2.0 to testnet, has a CVV number or a voting token in the selection tool's list.

4. **CVV005's schedule and partial burn.** The proposal planned mainnet "In 2 weeks after Testnet deployment" and a partial burn "in Casper v2.2" ([CVV005](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv005-proposal-enable-burning-mechanism/1538), read 2026-09-23). The burn reached mainnet on 2025-12-11, about twelve weeks after the vote closed. v2.2.2 has no partial-burn option ([fee_handling.rs#L16-L34](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/chainspec/fee_handling.rs#L16-L34)).

5. **CVV006 and reservations.** The proposal says the minimum "doesn't impact/limit the reservation delegation rate" ([CVV006](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv006-proposal-implement-minimum-validator-fee-mechanism/1539), read 2026-09-23). At v2.2.2 a reservation whose rate is below the minimum fails with `DelegationRateTooSmall`, as `add_bid` does ([detail.rs#L939-L941](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction/detail.rs#L939-L941), [auction.rs#L95-L97](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L95-L97)). With the mainnet minimum at 0 the difference has no effect yet.

6. **CVV008's base: circulating supply or total supply.** The proposal says "A one-time issuance of 33% of circulating supply" ([CVV008](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv008-proposal-securing-validator-economics-and-network-sustainability/1567), read 2026-09-23). The implementation computes 33% of 14,433,609,569.45 CSPR, which `global_state.toml` calls "the supply at block 6972606" ([global_state.toml#L6-L8](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/global_state.toml#L6-L8)). casper.network calls the result "a one-time issuance equal to 33% of the total CSPR supply" ([casper.network, 23 Mar 2026](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-2-2-0-goes-live-on-mainnet), read 2026-09-23). The white paper of 2025-09-15 had put circulating supply at 96.6% of total supply ([p. 17](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/668fef77d8ac075ed5e3f57a/68da95d664a37c9e855f816d_Whitepaper%20V3%20Submission.pdf#page=17)), so the two bases are not the same number.

7. **CVV007 and CVV010 contradict themselves on node counts and dates.** Both cap participants at "50 validators and 5 backup validators (full nodes)" and then select "50 validators and 3 full nodes". CVV007 also puts the lottery on "January 26, 2026" in one place and "January 29, 2026" in its timeline, and KYC "from January 26 to January 30, 2025" against "January 29 to January 31, 2026" ([CVV007](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv007-incentivized-testnet-proposal-casper-network-testnet-rewards-program-feb-01-june-30-2026/1560), [CVV010](https://forum.casper.network/t/cvv010-incentivized-testnet-proposal-casper-network-testnet-rewards-program-july-01-dec-31-2026/1575), read 2026-09-23).

8. **The selection tool lags the policy.** At f8775bb it implements CVV002's on-chain eligibility filters, without the top-10 exclusion. It does not check the Telegram-group, forum-status or contact criteria. It implements neither CVV009's allocation nor its testnet requirement ([fetch_validators.py#L111-L124](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/validator-selection-tool/blob/f8775bb68c54c9ff71d55d4ba48b256d00978440/fetch_validators.py#L111-L124); see the delegation section).
