# Casper Wallet: setup, recovery, dApps, Ledger, swap and scam safety

Verified against casper-wallet v2.8.0, casper-node v2.2.2 and docs.casper.network 2.0.0 on 2026-09-23.

Casper Wallet is a self-custodial wallet for the Casper Network, built by MAKE, available as a browser extension and a mobile app ([Casper 101: How to Create a Casper Wallet](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-create-a-casper-wallet), [Introducing Casper Wallet by MAKE](https://www.casper.network/news/introducing-casper-wallet-by-make), both read 2026-09-23). The same how-to explains the term: "self-custodial means that you are the only one who controls your wallet and funds, and not a third party" ([Casper 101: How to Create a Casper Wallet](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-create-a-casper-wallet), read 2026-09-23). casper.network's announcement calls it "a new Casper blockchain web wallet, built by MAKE, with support from the Casper Association and Casper Labs" and "The successor to Casper Signer" ([Introducing Casper Wallet by MAKE](https://www.casper.network/news/introducing-casper-wallet-by-make), read 2026-09-23; the page shows no date). The extension's source is public under the Apache License 2.0 ([README.md#L271-L273](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/README.md?plain=1#L271-L273)). This guide covers how to install it safely, what the recovery phrase is, what can and cannot be recovered, how connecting to a dApp and signing work, how Ledger, swap and wrap work, and how the scams aimed at wallet users work.

Three kinds of source appear below, and they are not equally durable. What the wallet does is pinned to the release tag [`v2.8.0`](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/tree/v2.8.0) of `make-software/casper-wallet` (commit `034ad8ea143fb2c4a45328e5a7a0266d6c40c2c5`, tagged 2026-09-21) and to [`v2.0.0`](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/tree/v2.0.0) of `make-software/casper-wallet-core` (commit `98d60955777a930cfb3651f7a98f81cc6aa57bba`), the version that release's lock file resolves ([package-lock.json#L8221-L8225](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/package-lock.json#L8221-L8225)); quoted interface text is the English text in that source. The release notes are the repository's [GitHub releases](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases): 44 of them on 2026-09-23, from [v1.4.0](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v1.4.0) of 2023-07-21 to [v2.8.0](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v2.8.0) of 2026-09-21. Those version numbers are the extension's: the iOS app numbers its own releases, and its 2.8.0 is a different release (next section). Pages on casper.network, docs.cspr.click, Telegram and the extension and app stores were read on **2026-09-23** and can change without a release; they are dated wherever they are quoted. The user guides the wallet's menu links to, at casperwallet.io ([constants.ts#L34](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/constants.ts#L34)), were not read for this guide.

## Creating a new wallet in the extension

The extension's onboarding, as the v2.8.0 source builds it:

1. **Password.** "To ensure a strong password use at least 16 characters. Think of a random and memorable passphrase. Avoid using personally identifiable information." ([create-vault-password/content.tsx#L34-L42](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/create-vault-password/content.tsx#L34-L42)); 16 is the enforced minimum, `minPasswordLength = 16` ([form-validation-rules.ts#L29-L41](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/ui/forms/form-validation-rules.ts#L29-L41)). The password unlocks the wallet; it is not the recovery phrase ([Casper 101](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-create-a-casper-wallet), read 2026-09-23: "This password will be used to unlock your wallet.").
2. **What the phrase is for.** "Your unique secret recovery phrase allows you to recover your wallet at any time on any device that has Casper Wallet installed." ([create-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L27-L30](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/create-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L27-L30)).
3. **Rules to accept.** Before generating the phrase the wallet lists "Save a backup in multiple secure locations.", "Never share the phrase with anyone.", "Be careful of phishing! Casper Wallet will never spontaneously ask you for your secret recovery phrase.", "If you need to back up your secret recovery phrase again, you can find it in Settings." and "Casper Wallet cannot recover your secret recovery phrase! If you lose it, you may not be able to recover your funds." ([create-secret-phrase-confirmation/content.tsx#L14-L35](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/create-secret-phrase-confirmation/content.tsx#L14-L35)), and continues only after you tick "I understand that I am solely responsible for storing and protecting my secret recovery phrase. Access to my funds depend on it." ([index.tsx#L48-L60](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/create-secret-phrase-confirmation/index.tsx#L48-L60)).
4. **Write it down.** The phrase is generated from 256 bits of entropy with the BIP-39 English word list ([bip39.ts#L6-L9](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/crypto/bip39.ts#L6-L9)), which makes 24 words ([BIP-39#L57-L65](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/0d1b892ddb21c22def4af4541bfed7a2a3480e4a/bip-0039.mediawiki?plain=1#L57-L65)). The screen says "Never disclose your secret recovery phrase. Anyone with this phrase can take your funds forever." ([write-down-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L37-L44](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/write-down-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L37-L44)). Casper 101 adds "Write the phrase down and store it somewhere safe and offline." and "Do not take a screenshot or save it on a cloud drive." (read 2026-09-23).
5. **Confirm it.** "Choose the six words one by one to complete the secret recovery phrase" ([confirm-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L41-L48](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/confirm-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L41-L48)).
6. **Done.** "Remember, if you ever lose access to your account you can always reinstall Casper Wallet and import your secret recovery phrase to regain access. Store it in a safe place." ([onboarding-success/content.tsx#L25-L33](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/onboarding-success/content.tsx#L25-L33)).

- **The mobile app** asks for "a 6-digit passcode you will use every time you open your wallet" instead of a password, then shows and confirms the phrase the same way ([Casper 101](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-create-a-casper-wallet), read 2026-09-23).

- **One phrase, many accounts.** "Create account" adds an account derived from the same phrase: the wallet derives account number *n* at the BIP-44 path `m/44'/506'/0'/0/n`, 506 being Casper's registered coin type ([bip44.ts#L1-L13](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/crypto/bip44.ts#L1-L13)), as a secp256k1 key ([bip32.ts#L9-L33](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/crypto/bip32.ts#L9-L33)) whose public key is written with the prefix `02` ([utils.ts#L5-L10](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/crypto/utils.ts#L5-L10)). So every account the wallet creates has a public key starting `02`; that public key is the address you give out to receive CSPR ([Casper 101](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-create-a-casper-wallet), read 2026-09-23: "You can share it with others to receive tokens.").

## The secret recovery phrase: what it is and who may see it

- **It is every key the wallet created.** Each account's key pair is computed from the phrase and the account's number ([bip32.ts#L9-L33](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/crypto/bip32.ts#L9-L33)), which is why the wallet says "Anyone with this phrase can take your funds forever." ([write-down-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L37-L44](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/write-down-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L37-L44)).

- **Nobody legitimate asks for it.** "Casper Wallet will never spontaneously ask you for your secret recovery phrase." ([create-secret-phrase-confirmation/content.tsx#L17-L22](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/create-secret-phrase-confirmation/content.tsx#L17-L22)), and for key files: "Casper Wallet will never spontaneously ask you for your private key files." ([download-account-keys/utils.ts#L33-L39](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/download-account-keys/utils.ts#L33-L39)). The documentation's rule for receiving funds: "Only use your **public key**. Never share your **private key** or **recovery phrase**." ([funding-from-exchanges.md#L78](https://github.com/casper-network/docs-redux/blob/c78e3c2040d30fd8c42cb95bc8a3b308db9d1a56/versioned_docs/version-2.0.0/users/funding-from-exchanges.md?plain=1#L78), [Funding Mainnet Accounts](https://docs.casper.network/users/funding-from-exchanges)).

- **Nobody else holds a copy.** "Casper Wallet does not keep a copy of your secret phrase." ([download-account-keys/utils.ts#L40-L45](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/download-account-keys/utils.ts#L40-L45)). It lives in the browser's extension storage as an encrypted vault: the wallet's storage inventory describes that record as "accounts, keys, and other secret vault contents", stored as "AES-256-GCM ciphertext" ([storage-keys.md#L27](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/docs/architecture/storage-keys.md?plain=1#L27)).

- **Seeing it again.** While you can still unlock the wallet, "Back up your secret recovery phrase" in the menu shows it after you enter your password ([backup-secret-phrase/index.tsx#L37](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/backup-secret-phrase/index.tsx#L37)); the wallet's advice there is again "Save a backup in multiple secure locations." and "Never share the phrase with anyone." ([backup-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L27-L47](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/backup-secret-phrase/content.tsx#L27-L47)).

- **The wallet QR code is the phrase, locked with your password.** "Generate wallet QR code" asks for the password ([wallet-qr-code/index.tsx#L103-L106](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/wallet-qr-code/index.tsx#L103-L106)), then encodes the secret recovery phrase, the account names and every imported account's secret key, encrypted with AES under a key derived from that password with scrypt ([generate-sync-wallet-qr-data-worker.ts#L19-L50](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/background/workers/generate-sync-wallet-qr-data-worker.ts#L19-L50)). A photo of the QR code plus the password therefore opens the whole wallet. Show it only to your own phone's Casper Wallet app ("Scan this with your Casper Wallet app.", [wallet-qr-code/content.tsx#L56](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/wallet-qr-code/content.tsx#L56)).

## Signing requests: what to check before you click Sign

- **Match the hash.** The documentation's transfer walkthrough says to "**check the deploy hash**. Ensure the deploy hash in the "Signature Request" window matches the deploy hash in the "Sign transaction" window before continuing." ([token-transfer.md#L36](https://github.com/casper-network/docs-redux/blob/c78e3c2040d30fd8c42cb95bc8a3b308db9d1a56/versioned_docs/version-2.0.0/users/csprlive/token-transfer.md?plain=1#L36), [Transfer Tokens](https://docs.casper.network/users/token-transfer)). Since v2.0.0 the wallet builds Casper 2.0 transactions ("All Deploys become Transactions", [v2.0.0](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v2.0.0)), so the same check applies to a transaction hash.

- **WASM transactions get an extra gate.** When a site asks you to sign a transaction that carries its own Wasm code, and you have not approved that code for that site before, the Sign button stays disabled until you tick "I initiated this transaction from a trusted application and understand the risks associated with executing WASM transactions." ([sign-transaction/index.tsx#L396-L408](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/signature-request/pages/sign-transaction/index.tsx#L396-L408), [#L467-L499](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/signature-request/pages/sign-transaction/index.tsx#L467-L499)). Ticking it records that code's hash as trusted for that site ([#L411-L440](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/signature-request/pages/sign-transaction/index.tsx#L411-L440)).

- **Why that box matters.** For a transaction that runs code through a proxy, the wallet explains: "This WASM transaction runs code from the smart contract shown below. However, Casper Wallet can’t check what the code inside the WASM actually does." ([unknown-contract-action-rows.tsx#L309-L327](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/signature-request/pages/sign-transaction/unknown-contract-action-rows.tsx#L309-L327)). The wallet's core library says of the proxy code its own swaps use that it runs "as session code in the caller's account context, with access to their main purse" and that "neither the wallet UI nor the Ledger prompt shows the user more than "ModuleBytes"" ([swap-react-integration.md#L107-L111](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/docs/swap-react-integration.md?plain=1#L107-L111)). That holds for any session code, not only the wallet's: the documentation says it "runs within the context of the account executing the session code. As a result, the session code runs with the account's permissions, such as having access to the account's main purse. For example, the session code could transfer tokens from the account's main purse." ([contract-vs-session.md#L7](https://github.com/casper-network/docs-redux/blob/c78e3c2040d30fd8c42cb95bc8a3b308db9d1a56/versioned_docs/version-2.0.0/developers/writing-onchain-code/contract-vs-session.md?plain=1#L7), [Contracts and Session Code](https://docs.casper.network/developers/writing-onchain-code/contract-vs-session)).

- **Other kinds of request.** A site can also ask the wallet to sign a message, EIP-712 typed data ([v2.5.0](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v2.5.0)) or to decrypt a message ([v2.3.0](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v2.3.0)); each opens its own request window.

## Ledger with Casper Wallet

- **What is supported.** Ledger arrived in [v1.10.0](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v1.10.0): "connect your ledger via USB or Bluetooth, transfer tokens, NFTs, delegate CSPR, sign deploys/messages from dapps". [v2.0.4](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v2.0.4) "Added support for signing TransactionV1 with Ledger", and [v2.8.0](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v2.8.0) moved Ledger onto Ledger's Device Management Kit: "A locked device reads as "waiting" instead of an error, and a signature the device left half-finished continues where it stopped once you unlock", and "Ledger is supported for swaps end to end."

- **Which browsers.** The "Connect Ledger" menu item exists only in the Chrome and Edge builds ([utils.ts#L47-L49](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/utils.ts#L47-L49), [navigation-menu/index.tsx#L191-L204](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/navigation-menu/index.tsx#L191-L204)). It offers "Connect via USB" and "Connect via Bluetooth" ([ledger-disconnected-footer.tsx#L58-L104](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/ui/components/ledger-footer/ledger-disconnected-footer.tsx#L58-L104)). The phone app is separate: the iOS app's Version History lists "native Ledger hardware wallet support: securely connect Ledger devices (Bluetooth/USB)" in its 2.4.0 of 2025-11-13 ([App Store: Casper Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/casper-wallet/id6446363274), read 2026-09-23).

- **The Casper app on the device.** Install it from Ledger Live's "My Ledger" catalogue; the documentation's tip: "Please search "Casper," not "CSPR"" ([ledger-setup.md#L22-L61](https://github.com/casper-network/docs-redux/blob/c78e3c2040d30fd8c42cb95bc8a3b308db9d1a56/versioned_docs/version-2.0.0/users/ledger/ledger-setup.md?plain=1#L22-L61), [Set up Ledger](https://docs.casper.network/workflow/ledger-setup)). The wallet needs it open ("Casper app isn’t open on Ledger", [ledger/errors.ts#L52-L56](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/services/ledger/errors.ts#L52-L56)), and an app older than 3.0.0 cannot sign Casper 2.0 transactions: "Only Deploy signature is supported for Ledger devices before v3.0.0." ([ledger/errors.ts#L70-L74](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/services/ledger/errors.ts#L70-L74)). The newest release of the app's source, `Zondax/ledger-casper`, was [v3.1.8](https://github.com/Zondax/ledger-casper/releases/tag/v3.1.8) of 2026-05-26 (GitHub API, read 2026-09-23).

- **Keep the device's recovery phrase off the computer.** The announcement of the wallet promised a Ledger integration "enabling users to connect their Ledger with Casper Wallet without requiring the secret recovery phrase" ([Introducing Casper Wallet by MAKE](https://www.casper.network/news/introducing-casper-wallet-by-make), read 2026-09-23), which is what "Connect Ledger" does. The same announcement claims the wallet's phrases are "compatible with Ledger, so users can use the same 24 words on Ledger and vice versa"; this guide did not verify that, and typing a Ledger's words into "Restore from a phrase" would store them in the browser vault described above.

## Swap, wrap and unwrap (extension v2.8.0)

- **What shipped.** "Swap tokens without leaving the wallet: pick a pair, see the rate, price impact and route, set your own slippage and deadline, then confirm. Swap is offered from Home and from a token's details page." and "CSPR and WCSPR wrap and unwrap through the same flow." ([v2.8.0 release notes](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v2.8.0), 2026-09-21).

- **Where the trades go.** Swaps are quoted and routed by CSPR.trade: the token picker lists the tokens CSPR.trade's API returns with `is_whitelisted: true` and `is_blacklisted: false` ([swap/index.ts#L55-L68](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/data/repositories/swap/index.ts#L55-L68), [useSwapTokens.ts#L61](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/react/hooks/swap/useSwapTokens.ts#L61)), and the extension treats that list as the whole answer to what can be swapped ("`dexTokens` is already the whitelist", [swap-service/utils.ts#L30](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/services/swap-service/utils.ts#L30)); quotes come from the same API's `/quote` ([swap/index.ts#L32-L47](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/data/repositories/swap/index.ts#L32-L47)), and the trade API is `https://api.cspr.trade` on Mainnet and `https://api.testnet.cspr.trade` on Testnet ([casperNetwork.ts#L34-L39](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/domain/constants/casperNetwork.ts#L34-L39)). A token CSPR.trade has not verified carries the warning "CSPR.trade hasn't verified this token. Verify the contract address and token details before trading. Proceed only if you understand the risks." ([token-selector-modal.tsx#L185-L195](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/swap/components/token-selector-modal.tsx#L185-L195)).

- **Where Swap appears.** Only on Mainnet and Testnet, and not in the Safari build ([swap-service/utils.ts#L23-L28](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/services/swap-service/utils.ts#L23-L28)). This is the extension; the iOS app's Version History lists no swap up to its 2.8.0 of 2026-08-31 ([App Store: Casper Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/casper-wallet/id6446363274), read 2026-09-23).

- **Settings and warnings.** Slippage defaults to 3% and can be set from 0.01% to 50%; the deadline defaults to 20 minutes and can be set from 1 to 120 ([config.ts#L23-L28](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/domain/constants/config.ts#L23-L28)). The wallet warns "Too high slippage" at 10% or more and "Very high price impact" above 10% ([config.ts#L41-L42](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/domain/constants/config.ts#L41-L42), [swap-settings-modal/index.tsx#L59](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/swap/components/swap-settings-modal/index.tsx#L59), [swap-details.tsx#L93](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/swap/components/swap-details.tsx#L93), [parse-settings-input.ts#L35](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/swap/components/swap-settings-modal/parse-settings-input.ts#L35), [swap-details-utils.ts#L10](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/swap/swap-details-utils.ts#L10)). The review screen shows a fee row labelled with `SWAP_PROTOCOL_FEE`, which v2.0.0 of the core sets to `0.003`, that is 0.3% ([swap/utils.ts#L138-L144](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/swap/utils.ts#L138-L144), [config.ts#L40](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/domain/constants/config.ts#L40)). Rates and price impact are live quotes; this guide states none.

- **Who gets the 0.3%.** It is CSPR.trade's pool fee, not a separate wallet charge. The wallet's swap is one call, through the proxy-caller Wasm below, to the Router package `1dbac65585475fec53e5b1f9110923c8d232921702097e83105b36751d682186` at one of its `swap_*` entry points ([dex/index.ts#L228-L305](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/data/repositories/dex/index.ts#L228-L305), [casperNetwork.ts#L90-L96](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/domain/constants/casperNetwork.ts#L90-L96)); CSPR.trade's own SDK names that package as its Mainnet router ([cspr-trade-mcp config.ts#L21-L29](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/packages/sdk/src/config.ts#L21-L29)). In CSPR.trade's contract source, the Router charges `amount_in × 997 / 1000` at each pool on the route ([router.rs#L586-L628](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L586-L628)), and the fee stays in the pool for its liquidity providers, less about one sixth minted to the Factory's `fee_to` when that is set ([pair.rs#L498-L528](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/pair.rs#L498-L528)); this guide did not rebuild the deployed contract to compare it with that source. The wallet's fee row is 0.3% of the amount paid in, whatever the route ([form-step.tsx#L276](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/swap/form-step.tsx#L276), [src/utils/swap.ts#L49-L58](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/utils/swap.ts#L49-L58)), so on a route through more than one pool it shows less than the pools take in total.

- **Approval first, for tokens.** When the token you pay with is not CSPR and its allowance is too small, the flow sends an approval transaction, waits for it on chain, then sends the swap ([swapFlow.ts#L142-L211](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/data/flows/swapFlow.ts#L142-L211)).

- **Payment amounts.** The core attaches 5 CSPR to an approval, 30 CSPR to a swap, and 5 CSPR to a wrap or an unwrap ([config.ts#L31-L37](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/domain/constants/config.ts#L31-L37)); these are payment limits, not prices: casper-node v2.2.2's Mainnet chainspec returns 75% of the unused part of a payment to the sender (`refund_handling = { type = 'refund', refund_ratio = [75, 100] }`) and burns fees ([chainspec.toml#L108-L126](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L108-L126)), and a transaction whose execution fails gets no refund, because the node then counts its whole cost as consumed ([operations.rs#L865-L872](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/node/src/components/contract_runtime/operations.rs#L865-L872), [internal.rs#L100-L104](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/handle_payment/internal.rs#L100-L104)). Every swap, wrap and unwrap carries a proxy-caller Wasm that the extension ships inside itself and checks against the SHA-256 `6f25e7a3098d8301a36327ec17e70570004f30985e3682d61bd849ee4c24548d` before building the transaction; its comment says the bytes "came from the cspr.trade web app's own proxy_caller.wasm" ([proxy-wasm.ts#L1-L29](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/libs/services/swap-service/proxy-wasm.ts#L1-L29), [signing-repositories.ts#L37-L40](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/background/signing-repositories.ts#L37-L40)).

- **What WCSPR is.** Wrapped CSPR is a CEP-18 token, "name `Wrapped CSPR`, symbol `WCSPR`, `9` decimals"; "Depositing CSPR mints an equal amount of WCSPR, and withdrawing burns WCSPR to release the underlying CSPR." ([odradev/wcspr README.md#L6-L20](https://github.com/odradev/wcspr/blob/1e1b0c39fa884c84597cc7f0c258cb3b81aa8a37/README.md?plain=1#L6-L20)). The wallet core names the Mainnet WCSPR contract package `8df5d26790e18cf0404502c62ce5dc9025800ad6975c97466e20506c39c505b6` ([casperNetwork.ts#L98-L103](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet-core/blob/v2.0.0/src/domain/constants/casperNetwork.ts#L98-L103)), and the wallet's wrap screen code notes "a wrap is 1:1, so gas is the only cost" ([wrap-details.tsx#L21](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/swap/components/wrap-details.tsx#L21)).

## Scams aimed at Casper Wallet users

- **Anyone asking for your phrase, a key file or your wallet QR code is trying to take your funds.** The wallet "will never spontaneously ask you for your secret recovery phrase" or "your private key files" ([create-secret-phrase-confirmation/content.tsx#L17-L22](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/onboarding/pages/create-secret-phrase-confirmation/content.tsx#L17-L22), [download-account-keys/utils.ts#L33-L39](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/download-account-keys/utils.ts#L33-L39)), and the QR code decrypts to the phrase with your password (above). That holds for a "support agent", a "moderator", a "validator", a form, a bot or a "wallet sync" site.

- **Fake support.** None of the sources read for this guide names a Casper Wallet support desk or says that anyone from the wallet will message you first. The wallet's menu links user guides at `https://casperwallet.io/user-guide`, a feature-request board at `https://casper-wallet.canny.io/feature-requests` and "About us" at `https://make.services` ([constants.ts#L34-L37](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/constants.ts#L34-L37)); vulnerabilities go privately to `security@make.software` ([SECURITY.md#L5](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/SECURITY.md?plain=1#L5)); Casper's how-tos send questions to the Telegram channel `https://t.me/casperblockchain` ([Casper 101](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-create-a-casper-wallet), read 2026-09-23); and the extension's own "rate the app" screen, which can open when you press "Done" after a transfer ([transfer/index.tsx#L578-L591](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/transfer/index.tsx#L578-L591)), offers "Get in touch" after you answer "Not so much", which opens a message in the Telegram group `https://t.me/CSPRhub` ([rate-app/index.tsx#L54-L61](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/rate-app/index.tsx#L54-L61), [#L90-L105](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/apps/popup/pages/rate-app/index.tsx#L90-L105)). - **Impersonation is getting better.** In casper.network's recap of the Casper X Space held on April 28, 2026 (the page shows no publication date), Halborn's Gabi Urrutia advised retail users to "follow official accounts and trusted protocol communication channels rather than relying on influencers or second-hand information", and Casper Association's President and CTO Michael Steuer described being contacted on Telegram by what turned out to be a deepfake of a known business associate; he also "mentioned phishing attempts disguised as physical mail from fake hardware wallet providers" ([April 28, 2026 Casper X Space Recap](https://www.casper.network/news/april-28-2026-casper-x-space-recap-web3-security-with-michael-steuer-and-gabi-urrutia), read 2026-09-23).
