# Liquid staking and DeFi on Casper: sCSPR, WCSPR, CSPR.trade, Styks, Friendly Market and csprUSD

Verified against casper-node v2.2.2, docs.casper.network 2.0.0, liquid-staking-contracts 2026.07.20, casper-trade 2026.08.13, styks 2026.07.13, wcspr 2026.07.13, cspr-trade-mcp v0.6.0, docs.friendly.market 2025.09.12 and docs.astralbeam.io 2026.09.21 on 2026-09-23.

This guide covers the DeFi contracts running on Casper mainnet: the sCSPR liquid staking token and the Wise Lending front end that stakes into it, WCSPR (wrapped CSPR), the CSPR.trade exchange, the Styks price oracle, the Casper pages of Friendly Market's documentation, and the status of the csprUSD stablecoin. Each short answer below was checked on 2026-09-23. sCSPR is minted by one smart contract, `StakedCSPR`, whose source is [casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/tree/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41) and whose mainnet front end is Wise Lending's app at [casper.wiselending.com/liquid-staking](https://casper.wiselending.com/liquid-staking) ([Casper's post on dev.to, 2026-03-19](https://dev.to/casperblockchain/opening-the-playbook-why-were-open-sourcing-caspers-core-defi-infrastructure-3d9o)). Unstaked CSPR can be claimed 16 hours after an unstake, not "approximately 8 days" as the repository's README says: the deployed contract stores a claim time of 57,600,000 milliseconds, read from mainnet at block 9,028,343 on 2026-09-23, and the chain's own unbonding delay is 7 eras of 120 minutes ([mainnet chainspec#L27](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L27), [#L55](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L55)), 14 hours at the nominal era length; because the auction releases an undelegation at the end of the seventh era after the one it was made in, the protocol returns the CSPR 14 to 16 hours after the request at that length ([unbond.rs#L327-L339](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/system/auction/unbond.rs#L327-L339), [auction.rs#L570-L574](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L570-L574)). The deployed contract charged 1% of staking rewards on that date, not the 10% the repository's deploy script uses ([deploy.rs#L24](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-cli/src/deploy.rs#L24)). A CSPR.trade swap pays 0.3% of its input ([router.rs#L586-L598](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L586-L598)), and because the mainnet factory had a protocol-fee recipient set on 2026-09-23, about one sixth of that goes to the protocol rather than to liquidity providers ([pair.rs#L498-L528](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/pair.rs#L498-L528)). Styks publishes one feed, `CSPRUSD`, with a 30-minute heartbeat ([styks README.md#L7-L13](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/README.md#L7-L13)), and its last update before block 9,028,343 was at 16:30 UTC on 2026-09-23. The upgraded csprUSD, the version with CEP-3009 authorized transfers meant for x402, was on testnet only on 2026-09-23; its July 2026 announcement said it was "expected to reach Casper Mainnet later in Q3" ([Chainwire, 2026-07-29](https://chainwire.org/2026/07/29/csprusd-launching-as-caspers-standard-stablecoin-rebuilt-for-the-machine-economy/)). Mainnet held an older csprUSD contract, installed under protocol 1.5.6, at the package the x402 Casper library names for mainnet ([constants.ts#L40-L55](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/blob/71eb9a55e081e7b81ba3046d0bd17c3eb9c7bf81/typescript/packages/mechanisms/casper/src/constants.ts#L40-L55)).

## sCSPR staking: what `stake` does

`stake` is payable and takes no argument: the attached CSPR is the amount ([token.rs#L165-L193](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L165-L193)). In order, it:

1. reverts with Odra's `UnpausedRequired` (21001) if the contract is paused (see *sCSPR error codes*);
2. reverts with `StakeBelowMinimum` (61408) if the amount is under the minimum stake ([token.rs#L812-L816](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L812-L816)). The deployed minimum was 500,000,000,000 motes, 500 CSPR, at block 9,028,343 on 2026-09-23, the same as the chain's `minimum_delegation_amount` ([mainnet chainspec#L72](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L72));
3. collects the owner's fee on rewards earned since the last recorded stake (see *sCSPR fees* below);
4. delegates the whole amount to one validator picked from the contract's validator list with host-provided pseudorandom bytes ([token.rs#L751-L766](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L751-L766));
5. mints `cspr_amount × total_sCSPR_supply ÷ staked_cspr` sCSPR to the caller, or one sCSPR per CSPR when nothing is staked yet ([token.rs#L627-L646](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L627-L646)), and emits `Staked { address, cspr_amount, scspr_minted }` ([events.rs#L6-L15](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/events.rs#L6-L15)).

A user therefore receives fewer sCSPR than the CSPR they stake once the rate has risen above one. Because `stake` carries CSPR, the repository's tools send it through a proxy-caller Wasm: its CLI logs `Calling "hash-462a…" with entrypoint "stake" through proxy.` ([README.md#L72-L80](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/README.md#L72-L80)), and its JavaScript client passes `--proxy_caller ../wasm/proxy_caller.wasm` to the `stake` script and to neither `unstake` nor `claim` ([js-cli/README.md#L45-L58](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/js-cli/README.md#L45-L58), [#L107-L119](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/js-cli/README.md#L107-L119), [#L180-L191](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/js-cli/README.md#L180-L191)). The user-facing steps are on Casper's how-to page: connect a wallet on the Wise Lending app, enter an amount in the Stake section, review "how much sCSPR you will receive, the transaction payment, and the exchange rate", and sign ([casper.network: Casper 101: How to Use Liquid Staking on Casper](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-use-liquid-staking-on-casper), read 2026-09-23).

Delegating from Casper Wallet is not this. The wallet's "Delegate" sends the auction's own `delegate` for your account, so the stake stays a delegation held by your account, taken from its main purse, to a validator you pick, and no token is minted ([constants.ts#L162-L175](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/blob/v2.8.0/src/constants.ts#L162-L175), [auction.rs#L308-L345](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L308-L345)); an undelegation is paid back into the account's main purse by the auction itself after the 7-era delay described under *sCSPR unstaking and claiming*, with no claim to send ([auction_native.rs#L303-L377](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction/auction_native.rs#L303-L377)). For that native delegation, the section *Staking with Casper Wallet* of the guide [Casper Wallet: setup, recovery, dApps, Ledger, swap and scam safety](casper-wallet.md) in this repository gives the wallet's delegate, undelegate and redelegate steps, the minimums and the unbonding time.

## sCSPR exchange rate: how it is computed

- **The formula.** The CSPR behind all sCSPR is `staked_cspr`: the sum of the contract's delegations to every validator in its list, plus a `removed_validator_stake` counter for stake that is on its way back from a validator ([token.rs#L314-L328](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L314-L328)). Redeeming `n` sCSPR yields `n × staked_cspr ÷ total_sCSPR_supply` CSPR ([token.rs#L648-L671](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L648-L671)); the README states the same as `exchange_rate = total_staked_cspr / total_scspr_supply` ([liquid-staking-contracts/README.md#L61-L81](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/README.md#L61-L81)).
- **Why it rises.** Staking rewards are added to the contract's delegations, so `staked_cspr` grows while the sCSPR supply does not; the repository's test "Staking with rewards and fee collection" shows 1000 CSPR staked becoming 1000.000099999 CSPR after one auction ([staking.feature#L63-L70](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/tests/features/staking.feature#L63-L70)). The client README says the rate "is updated every two hours, coinciding with the arrival of rewards from the Casper auction contract" and that "staking or unstaking actions do not affect this rate directly" ([js-cli/README.md#L11](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/js-cli/README.md#L11)); two hours is one era on mainnet ([mainnet chainspec#L27](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L27)).
- **The value on 2026-09-23.** At block 9,028,343 the contract's single delegation held 19,449,658.333714981 CSPR and the sCSPR supply was 18,037,619.218173489, so one sCSPR redeemed for about 1.0783 CSPR (both values read with `query_global_state` on 2026-09-23; `removed_validator_stake` had no stored value). The figure changes every era. It is the contract's redemption rate, not the price sCSPR trades at on CSPR.trade, which is set by that pool's reserves; this guide does not quote market prices. The rate shown in the Wise Lending app is labelled "Exchange Rate: 1 sCSPR = … CSPR" ([casper.wiselending.com bundle](https://casper.wiselending.com/static/js/main.a076da9e.js), read 2026-09-23).

## sCSPR unstaking and claiming: 16 hours, not 8 days

Unstaking is two calls, and the second is not automatic.

1. **`unstake(scspr_amount)`** ([token.rs#L195-L254](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L195-L254)) reverts if the contract is paused or the amount is zero (`InvalidUnstakeAmount`, 61417), collects the fee, converts the sCSPR to CSPR at the current rate, and undelegates that CSPR starting from a pseudorandomly chosen validator and moving on to the next until the amount is covered ([token.rs#L768-L810](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L768-L810)). If the delegations cannot cover it the call reverts with `InsufficientBalance` (61406). It then burns the sCSPR, records an `UnstakingInfo { unstake_id, owner, cspr_amount, claimable_from, claimed }` whose `claimable_from` is the block time plus the contract's `claim_time`, and emits `Unstaked` with the id and that time ([token.rs#L57-L72](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L57-L72), [token.rs#L623-L625](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L623-L625)). There is no minimum for an unstake beyond "not zero" ([js-cli/README.md#L17](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/js-cli/README.md#L17)).
2. **`claim()`** takes no argument ([token.rs#L256-L312](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L256-L312)). It walks all of the caller's unstake requests, skips any whose `claimable_from` is still in the future or that is already claimed, marks the rest claimed, and pays each from the contract's own purse with `transfer_tokens`, emitting one `Claimed` event per request. A caller with no matured request pays for the transaction and receives nothing. The Wise Lending app calls this step "Withdraw", under a "Withdrawable" section ([casper.network: Casper 101: How to Use Liquid Staking on Casper](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-use-liquid-staking-on-casper), read 2026-09-23). Pending requests can be listed with `get_unstake_ids(account)` and `get_unstake(id)` ([token.rs#L589-L597](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L589-L597)).

"8 eras" and "16 hours" agree, since 8 × 120 minutes is 16 hours; "approximately 8 days" is the error. The app's and Casper's "7-eras (~16 hours)" joins two different numbers: 7 eras is the protocol's unbonding delay, 14 hours at the nominal 120-minute era, while 16 hours is the contract's own `claim_time`, 8 nominal eras, and is the wait a user sees before Withdraw. How the 7-era delay becomes 14 to 16 hours: an undelegation records the era it was made in ([detail.rs#L553](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction/detail.rs#L553)); `run_auction`, which moves the auction from one era to the next ([auction.rs#L637](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L637), [#L655](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L655)), first releases every request for which the era that is ending is at least its era plus `unbonding_delay` ([auction.rs#L570-L574](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L570-L574), [detail.rs#L479-L487](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction/detail.rs#L479-L487), [unbond.rs#L327-L339](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/system/auction/unbond.rs#L327-L339)). A request made during era N is therefore paid out at the end of era N+7, between 7 and 8 eras after it was made. The live chainspec answered by `info_get_chainspec` on 2026-09-23 was byte-for-byte the v2.2.2 mainnet file cited here. Three details matter to anyone waiting:

- The contract's timer is wall-clock block time in milliseconds, not eras ([token.rs#L94-L95](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L94-L95), [#L623-L625](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L623-L625)). The protocol's delay is counted in eras, and an era "will take longer than `era_duration` if that is necessary to reach the minimum height" of 100 blocks ([mainnet chainspec#L26-L30](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L26-L30)). `claim` pays from the contract's purse and does not itself check that the protocol has released the undelegated CSPR; the 16-hour timer is what separates the two. Those 16 hours equal the protocol's longest release time at the nominal era length (8 eras, for a request made at the very start of an era), so there is no margin when eras run longer than 120 minutes.
- The owner can change `claim_time` with `set_claim_time` ([token.rs#L517-L521](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L517-L521)). A change applies to later unstakes only, because each request stores its own `claimable_from` when it is made.
- A paused contract refuses `claim` as well as `stake` and `unstake`, with Odra's `UnpausedRequired` (21001) ([token.rs#L259](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L259)).

## sCSPR fees: 1% of rewards on mainnet on 2026-09-23

- **How the fee is taken.** Before each stake, unstake or validator removal, `collect_fee` compares the current delegated total with the last recorded one. If it grew, the growth is treated as reward, `fee = reward × fee_percentage ÷ 10000`, and that fee is minted to the owner as new sCSPR at the current rate ([token.rs#L673-L710](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L673-L710)). Holders pay it through a slightly lower redemption rate, not through a deduction from their balance. The contract charges no fee on the amount staked or unstaked.
- **The deployed rate.** `fee_percentage` was 100 basis points, 1% of rewards, at mainnet block 9,028,343 on 2026-09-23. The repository's deploy script sets `fee_percentage: 1000.into()`, 10% ([deploy.rs#L24](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-cli/src/deploy.rs#L24)), the README's example uses 10% ([README.md#L47-L49](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/README.md#L47-L49)), and the testnet contract held 1000 on testnet block 9,269,822 the same day. Quote the on-chain value, with its date, rather than either document.
- **It can change.** The owner can set any value up to `MAX_FEE_PERCENTAGE = 10000`, which is 100% of rewards ([token.rs#L16](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L16), [token.rs#L528-L535](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/src/token.rs#L528-L535)); the repository's test "Owner can set the fee_percentage" changes it from 1000 to 2000 ([configuration.feature#L3-L15](https://github.com/casper-ecosystem/liquid-staking-contracts/blob/26b4832ae4e2048822270b7181f067300f6adb41/liquid-staking-contracts/tests/features/configuration.feature#L3-L15)).
- **The validator's commission comes first.** Delegators collect rewards "net of a cut ("delegation rate") that is collected by the validator" ([glossary D.md#L13-L19](https://github.com/casper-network/docs-redux/blob/c78e3c2040d30fd8c42cb95bc8a3b308db9d1a56/versioned_docs/version-2.0.0/concepts/glossary/D.md#L13-L19)), and the contract is a delegator. The one validator in the contract's list on 2026-09-23 had `"delegation_rate": 3` in its bid (`query_global_state` on `bid-addr-01…`, block 9,028,343), a 3% cut.

## WCSPR: wrapped CSPR

- **What it is.** "Wrapped CSPR (WCSPR) — a CEP-18 (ERC-20 style) wrapper for the native CSPR token", where "Depositing CSPR mints an equal amount of WCSPR, and withdrawing burns WCSPR to release the underlying CSPR" ([wcspr README.md#L1-L8](https://github.com/odradev/wcspr/blob/1e1b0c39fa884c84597cc7f0c258cb3b81aa8a37/README.md#L1-L8)). `deposit` is payable and mints exactly the attached amount; `withdraw(amount)` burns and sends CSPR back, depositing into a contract caller rather than transferring; `withdraw_to(recipient, amount)` sends it elsewhere ([wcspr_v1.rs#L42-L99](https://github.com/odradev/wcspr/blob/1e1b0c39fa884c84597cc7f0c258cb3b81aa8a37/src/wcspr_v1.rs#L42-L99)). The source has no owner, no pause and no mint other than `deposit` ([wcspr_v1.rs#L26-L116](https://github.com/odradev/wcspr/blob/1e1b0c39fa884c84597cc7f0c258cb3b81aa8a37/src/wcspr_v1.rs#L26-L116)).
- **V2 adds CEP-3009.** `WCSPRV2` "composes the original token with the `CEP3009` module to add authorization-based (gasless / meta) transfers": `transfer_with_authorization`, `receive_with_authorization`, `cancel_authorization` and `authorization_state` ([wcspr README.md#L29-L41](https://github.com/odradev/wcspr/blob/1e1b0c39fa884c84597cc7f0c258cb3b81aa8a37/README.md#L29-L41), [wcspr_v2.rs#L26-L58](https://github.com/odradev/wcspr/blob/1e1b0c39fa884c84597cc7f0c258cb3b81aa8a37/src/wcspr_v2.rs#L26-L58)). CEP-3009 is the Casper proposal for "off-chain authorization of CEP-18 token transfers via signed messages" ([CEP-3009](https://github.com/casper-network/ceps/blob/3748c9017218378e650d8e5731feaf82088657bd/text/3009-transfer-with-authorization.md)).
- **On mainnet on 2026-09-23.** Package `8df5d267…05b6` had three versions, the first two disabled; the live one exposed the four CEP-3009 entry points beside `deposit`, `withdraw` and `withdraw_to`, reported name `Wrapped CSPR`, symbol `WCSPR` and 9 decimals, and stored `chain_name` `casper:casper` (block 9,028,343, read 2026-09-23). Its total supply, 5,839,171.356107747 WCSPR, equalled its main purse's balance of 5,839,171.356107747 CSPR at that block. The repository says the deploy script registers the package under the named key `WrappedNativeToken`, which "must match the one used on testnet and mainnet" ([wcspr README.md#L104-L108](https://github.com/odradev/wcspr/blob/1e1b0c39fa884c84597cc7f0c258cb3b81aa8a37/README.md#L104-L108)), and that `WCSPRV1` was ported from Odra's `WrappedNativeToken` module ([22b73cd](https://github.com/odradev/wcspr/commit/22b73cdccb4677fdac9cd477806c4e682d9b5807)).
- **Who deployed it.** CSPR.trade's token API gives the package's `owner_public_key` as `010f6740b4acf93bedd2525b3e4394ddc342a06aa14278857d81b910a6b8adbfe7` (`https://api.cspr.trade/tokens`, read 2026-09-23). That key's account hash, `b2480ab6…57da`, is also the CSPR.trade Factory's protocol-fee recipient (see below).
- **Where it is used.** CSPR.trade's Router holds WCSPR and wraps and unwraps CSPR for its `*_cspr` entry points ([router.rs#L25-L28](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L25-L28), [router.rs#L135](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L135)); the Router's stored WCSPR address on 2026-09-23 was this package. Casper Wallet v2.8.0, released 2026-09-21, added "CSPR and WCSPR wrap and unwrap through the same flow" ([Casper Wallet v2.8.0](https://github.com/make-software/casper-wallet/releases/tag/v2.8.0)). On AstralBeam's testnet, wCSPR is testnet package `3d80df21…7c1e` ([docs.astralbeam.io: Supported Tokens](https://docs.astralbeam.io/using-astralbeam/supported-tokens), read 2026-09-23), the same hash the CSPR.trade SDK uses for testnet ([config.ts#L11-L19](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/packages/sdk/src/config.ts#L11-L19)). ## CSPR.trade: contracts, swap fee and liquidity

- **What it is.** "A decentralized exchange (DEX) implementation based on Uniswap V2, built for the Casper Network using the Odra framework"; "The implementation tries to follow the Uniswap V2 implementation 1 to 1" ([casper-trade README.md#L1-L13](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/README.md#L1-L13)). The contracts are `Pair`, `PairFactory` (generated by Odra's `factory=on` directive, because Casper has no `create2`), `Router` and `Factory` ([README.md#L15-L47](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/README.md#L15-L47), [Odra.toml](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/Odra.toml)). Casper's ecosystem page says "Built by: MAKE" ([casper.network: CSPR.trade](https://www.casper.network/ecosystem-project/cspr-trade), read 2026-09-23); the Association's recap of its end-of-2025 X Space says "a Casper-native decentralized exchange built by Odra and MAKE", live on testnet then, with "Mainnet launch … targeted for January 2026" ([casper.network, end-of-year X Space recap](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-end-of-year-x-space-recap-2025-in-review-new-dex-announced-live-and-the-path-forward), read 2026-09-23).
- **The swap fee is 0.3%.** The Router quotes `amount_in × 997 / 1000` as the effective input ([router.rs#L586-L598](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L586-L598), [#L600-L614](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L600-L614)), and the Pair enforces it independently: "Check K invariant with 0.3% fee", `balance * 1000 - amount_in * 3`, reverting with `K` (20010) when the adjusted product falls below the old one ([pair.rs#L339-L348](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/pair.rs#L339-L348)). The fee is not sent anywhere at swap time; it stays in the pool's reserves. Casper's CSPR.trade playbook describes it as "Trading Fee – A small percentage (typically 0.3%) charged on each trade and distributed to LPs" ([casper.network: CSPR.trade Open Beta Playbook](https://www.casper.network/get-started/cspr-trade-open-beta-playbook), read 2026-09-23), which leaves out the protocol's share described next.
- **Part of the fee goes to the protocol.** When the Factory's `fee_to` is set, each `mint` and `burn` first mints LP tokens to `fee_to` equal to "1/6th of the growth in sqrt(k)" since the last liquidity event ([pair.rs#L498-L528](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/pair.rs#L498-L528)); growth in `k` between liquidity events comes from the swap fee, so this is about one sixth of the 0.3%, leaving liquidity providers about 0.25 percentage points. On 2026-09-23 the mainnet Factory's `fee_to` was set, to `account-hash-b2480ab6a679b3dca7318c71cbf0565a64db34de103bb2861114c401e99b57da`, and the WCSPR/sCSPR pair's `k_last` was non-zero, which the code only keeps while the fee is on (block 9,028,343). Only a Factory admin can change `fee_to` ([factory.rs#L65-L75](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/factory.rs#L65-L75), [factory.rs#L149-L153](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/factory.rs#L149-L153)); the mainnet Factory exposed `register_admin`, `unregister_admin`, `is_admin` and `set_fee_to` on 2026-09-23.
- **Providing liquidity.** The first deposit into a pool mints `sqrt(amount0 × amount1)` LP tokens less `MINIMUM_LIQUIDITY` = 1000 units, which are minted to the zero address and locked for good; later deposits mint in proportion to the smaller side's share ([pair.rs#L23](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/pair.rs#L23), [pair.rs#L158-L207](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/pair.rs#L158-L207)). Anyone can create a pair through the Factory ([factory.rs#L77-L121](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/factory.rs#L77-L121)); Casper's how-to says so in user terms: "go to the Pool page, click Create Liquidity Position, and create a new pair yourself" ([casper.network: How to Provide Liquidity on CSPR.trade](https://www.casper.network/get-started/casper-101-how-to-provide-liquidity-on-cspr-trade), read 2026-09-23). The same page warns: "Providing liquidity involves impermanent loss, which occurs when token prices change relative to each other."
- **Transaction payment.** The CSPR.trade SDK attaches these payment amounts: 5 CSPR for an approval, 30 CSPR for a swap, 50 CSPR for any add-liquidity transaction, including the first deposit into a new pair, and 30 CSPR for removing liquidity; its default slippage is 3% and its default deadline 20 minutes ([config.ts#L35-L49](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/packages/sdk/src/config.ts#L35-L49)). config.ts also defines a 500 CSPR `addNewLiquidity` constant that the SDK does not use: the add-liquidity builder always takes the 50 CSPR value, with the comment "caller can override if new pool", leaving the caller to raise the payment for a new pool ([client.ts#L765-L766](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/packages/sdk/src/client.ts#L765-L766)). These are payment limits the SDK sets, not what the network finally charges.
- **Tokens listed.** `https://api.cspr.trade/tokens` returned seven tokens on 2026-09-23: Wrapped CSPR, Staked CSPR, Casper Delta Long Token, Casper Delta Short Token and Chainmail Stamp with `"is_whitelisted": true`, and two test tokens, Ghostminter-Test3 and Ghostminter-Test1, with `false`. No stablecoin was in the list.
- **Audit.** Halborn reviewed commit `65efa8e` between 6 and 14 November 2025 and reported 9 findings, 1 critical, 1 medium, 1 low and 6 informational, all "Solved"; the critical one is titled "Missing access control on fee receiver allows protocol fee theft" ([Halborn](https://www.halborn.com/audits/casper-association/odradevcasper--trade-af1b5a), read 2026-09-23, "Last updated at 12/03/2025"). The repository's notes on each fix are in [Audit Report.md](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/tests/Audit%20Report.md).
- **For agents.** The CSPR.trade MCP server at `https://mcp.cspr.trade/mcp` runs on mainnet and builds unsigned transactions ([getting-started.md#L5-L15](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/docs/getting-started.md#L5-L15), [casper-2x-compatibility.md#L31-L36](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/docs/casper-2x-compatibility.md#L31-L36)). Its v0.6.0 getting-started page says it "exposes 24 public tools". That count includes `sign_deploy`, which the v0.6.0 code registers only in the separate `cspr-trade-signer` server, not in the public one ([server.ts#L19-L37](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/packages/mcp/src/server.ts#L19-L37)); the same release's llms.txt configures that signer as an optional local server and says "With both servers configured, the agent has 24 total tools across the full setup" ([llms.txt#L37-L56](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/docs/llms.txt#L37-L56)). On 2026-09-23 an MCP `initialize` and `tools/list` against the public endpoint reported server `cspr-trade` version 0.7.0, a version with no GitHub release or npm publish on that date, and listed 23 tools, `sign_deploy` not among them. Its `get_pairs` tool returns reserves ([llms.txt#L85-L91](https://github.com/make-software/cspr-trade-mcp/blob/v0.6.0/docs/llms.txt#L85-L91)); ask it, not this guide, for current liquidity.

### CSPR.trade error codes

From [router/errors.rs](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router/errors.rs), which holds two enums, the Router's own `RouterError` and the `LibraryError` of the quoting functions merged into the Router, and [pair/errors.rs](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/pair/errors.rs):

| Code | Name | Usual cause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 21001 | `Expired` | The deadline passed before the transaction ran |
| 21002, 21003 | `InsufficientAAmount`, `InsufficientBAmount` | Adding or removing liquidity would return less than the minimum set |
| 21004 | `InsufficientOutputAmount` | A swap would return less than the minimum set (slippage) |
| 21005 | `ExcessiveInputAmount` | An exact-output swap would need more input than the maximum set |
| 21006 | `InvalidPath` | A CSPR swap whose path does not start (CSPR in) or end (CSPR out) with WCSPR |
| 21007 | `PairNotFound` | No pool exists for a hop in a swap route, or for the pair a remove-liquidity call names |
| 20004 | `InsufficientLiquidityMinted` | A deposit too small to mint any LP token |
| 20010 | `K` | The pool invariant check failed |
| 4 | `InsufficientLiquidity` (library) | The pool has no reserves, or an exact-output swap asks for as much as the pool's reserve or more |
| 5, 6 | `InsufficientInputAmount`, `InsufficientOutputAmount` (library) | A swap or quote with a zero input amount, or an exact-output one with a zero output amount |
| 7 | `InvalidPath` (library) | A path of fewer than two tokens |

`InvalidPath` 21006 is raised only by the four `*_cspr` swaps, when the path does not start or end with WCSPR ([router.rs#L403-L406](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L403-L406), [#L446-L449](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L446-L449), [#L490-L493](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L490-L493), [#L534-L537](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L534-L537)); a missing pool is `PairNotFound` ([router.rs#L690-L695](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L690-L695)). The library codes 4 to 7 come from `quote`, `get_amount_out`, `get_amount_in`, `get_amounts_out` and `get_amounts_in`; every swap computes its amounts with `get_amounts_out` or `get_amounts_in` ([router.rs#L574-L643](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router.rs#L574-L643), [router/errors.rs#L16-L26](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/router/errors.rs#L16-L26)). The library's codes 1 to 3 are `IdenticalAddresses`, `ZeroAddress` and `InsufficientAmount`, and the Factory's own errors reuse 1 to 3, with 3 (`PermissionDenied`) for a caller that is not a Factory admin ([factory.rs#L148-L165](https://github.com/odradev/casper-trade/blob/485af535f1b1e17606a2c4e7e4fe4047869b81b8/casper_trade_contracts/src/factory.rs#L148-L165)), so these small numbers, like 20001 and 21001 (see *sCSPR error codes*), mean different things in different contracts.

## Styks: the price oracle

- **What it is.** "The very first onchain price oracle for Casper", deployed on mainnet with one feed, `CSPRUSD`, a 30-minute heartbeat, and the changelog line "2026/01/22 - Mainnet launch." ([styks README.md#L1-L25](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/README.md#L1-L25)). The README says "It is free to use!" ([README.md#L44](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/README.md#L44)) and "Initially, Odra.dev team will act as the `StyksAdmin`" ([README.md#L301-L307](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/README.md#L301-L307)). The Association's February 2026 recap says Styks "went live and was already tapped by multiple teams during the hackathon" ([casper.network, X Space recap 5 Feb 2026](https://www.casper.network/news/casper-x-space-recap-feb-5-2026-parking-mvp-goes-live-defi-expands-hackathon-winners-and-live-q-a), read 2026-09-23).
- **Reading it from a contract.** Call `get_twap_price` on `StyksPriceFeed` with the feed id; it returns `Option<u64>`, `None` when the feed is missing or has missed too many heartbeats ([README.md#L46-L71](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/README.md#L46-L71), [styks_price_feed.rs#L264-L294](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/styks-contracts/src/styks_price_feed.rs#L264-L294)). The value is a simple average of the stored prices in the window, not time-weighted despite the name ("It still has the `TW` in its name, even that it is a simple average", [README.md#L147-L167](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/README.md#L147-L167); [twap.rs#L55-L67](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/styks-core/src/twap.rs#L55-L67)), and it is `None` when fewer than `twap_window - twap_tolerance` of the window's heartbeats carried a price.
- **The mainnet configuration on 2026-09-23.** At block 9,028,343 the feed's stored config was `heartbeat_interval` 1800 seconds, `heartbeat_tolerance` 60 seconds, `twap_window` 3, `twap_tolerance` 1 and feeds `["CSPRUSD"]`, the values in the repository's configuration scenario ([make/set_config.rs#L42-L48](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/styks-cli/src/scenarios/make/set_config.rs#L42-L48)): the average of the last three half-hourly prices, still returned when one of the three is missing. Its `last_heartbeat` was 1790181000, which is 2026-09-23 16:30:00 UTC, about four and a half minutes before that block.
- **The unit is not documented.** The price is a bare `u64` ([styks-core lib.rs#L11-L12](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/styks-core/src/lib.rs#L11-L12)) and the README gives no scale. The supplier the feed is fed through parses the signed USD amount into an integer with five decimal places, "Multiply by 100,000 and take integer part" ([parser.rs#L48-L74](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/styks-make-parser/src/parser.rs#L48-L74)), while the CLI scenario that reports a price directly multiplies by 100,000,000 ([make/update_price.rs#L84-L94](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/styks-cli/src/scenarios/make/update_price.rs#L84-L94)). The values stored on mainnet on 2026-09-23 were consistent with the five-decimal scaling; confirm the scale before relying on it.
- **Where the prices come from.** A `PriceProducer` fetches signed prices from CSPR.cloud and posts them to the supplier contract, which checks them and forwards them to the feed ([README.md#L73-L122](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/README.md#L73-L122)). The deployed supplier, named `StyksMakeSupplier` in the repository's mainnet list and `StyksCsprCloudSupplier` in the README, verifies the signature against its configured public key, requires the reported timestamp to be within `timestamp_tolerance` of block time, and maps CSPR.cloud's currency id to a feed id ([make_supplier.rs#L96-L125](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/styks-contracts/src/make_supplier.rs#L96-L125), [#L156-L162](https://github.com/odradev/styks/blob/c9db96e65a6a9000e409454157ba3e2091cbd55a/styks-contracts/src/make_supplier.rs#L156-L162)).
