# What changed in Casper 2.1 and 2.2

Verified against casper-node v2.2.2 on 2026-09-10.

This page lists what changed in casper-node after v2.0.0 up to v2.2.2, the [latest release](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.2); the newest documentation version in docs-redux at commit [c78e3c2](https://github.com/casper-network/docs-redux/commit/c78e3c2040d30fd8c42cb95bc8a3b308db9d1a56) (dated 2025-09-28) is [2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/docs-redux/blob/c78e3c2040d30fd8c42cb95bc8a3b308db9d1a56/versions.json#L2). The mainnet chainspec at v2.2.2 declares [protocol 2.2.2 from era 22931](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L3-L14) and has an [8000 ms minimum block time](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L32), [burned fees](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L126), a [500 CSPR minimum bid](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L77), a [sustain ratio of 2/8](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L184) and a [`minimum_delegation_rate` of 0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L88-L89). Contract calls can also name a major protocol version ([v2.0.3](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.0.3)), and two releases were security releases ([v2.0.4](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.0.4), [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.2)).

Why it matters: a tag's `activation_point` is not proof of when mainnet upgraded.

## Consensus, block timing and capacity

- v2.1.0 set `minimum_block_time` from '16384 ms' to '8000 ms' and `minimum_era_height` from 20 to 100 ([v2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L28-L32), [v2.1.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.1.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L28-L32)). Its notes say the settings "optimize network operation at a block time of 8 seconds" ([v2.1.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.1.0)).
- Several per-block limits were cut to match; the v2.1.1 notes say the configuration "modifies settings to double windows and half capacity of blocks to keep throughput similar while dropping latency" ([v2.1.1](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.1.1)). `block_gas_limit` went from 1,625,000,000,000 to 812,500,000,000, and the per-block transaction counts of the native mint lane, the native auction lane and Wasm lane 5 went from 650, 650 and 80 to 325, 325 and 40 ([v2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L191-L223), [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L197-L229)). `max_block_size` went from 5,242,880 to 2,621,400 bytes, 40 bytes under half ([v2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L189), [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L195)). The install/upgrade lane, Wasm lanes 3 and 4 and `block_max_approval_count` (2600) did not change ([v2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L187-L222), [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L193-L228)).
- Why it matters: Transaction V1 native transfers and burns, and Deploy transfers, go to the mint lane ([transaction_v1.rs](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/transaction/transaction_v1.rs#L721-L723), [deploy.rs](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/transaction/deploy.rs#L1741-L1742)), so a block now holds at most 325 of them instead of 650.
- `signature_rewards_max_delay`, the lookback for rewarding finality signatures, went from 3 to 6 blocks ([v2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L92-L93), [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L94-L95)).
- v2.2.1 fixed a bug "which caused the validator matrix to not have enough validator awareness" when a node went down in one era and came back in another ([commit 978c3c2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/commit/978c3c2502f7c6cc681da366057ee36aae3d30bf)).

## Rewards, issuance and fees

- Issuance target is unchanged: both files say "Annual issuance: 8%", and v2.1.0 recomputed `round_seigniorage_rate` for 8000 ms blocks, from [7, 175070816] to [11, 563427926] ([v2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L56-L66), [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L56-L66)).
- Fees are burned: `fee_handling` changed from 'pay_to_proposer' ("fees are paid to the block proposer") to 'burn' ("fees are burned") ([v2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L116-L124), [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L118-L126)). The v2.1.0 notes call this the "Implementation of burn mechanism for fees on transaction" ([v2.1.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.1.0)), and the mainnet file has `fee_handling = { type = 'burn' }` ([mainnet](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L126)).
- `rewards_handling` (v2.2.0) is either `{ type = 'standard' }`, the pre-2.2.0 behavior, or `{ type = 'sustain', ratio = [a,b], purse_address = "uref-…" }`, which sends a ratio of newly minted rewards to an existing purse; a ratio whose numerator exceeds its denominator is rejected, and the purse is a regular, liquid purse ([v2.2.0 notes](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0), [code](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/chainspec/rewards_handling.rs#L19-L62)).
- A network that switches between the two modes "can switch back in the following upgrade" ([v2.2.0 notes](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0)). For a new network, `purse_address` "is ignored at genesis"; instead `accounts.toml` must contain an `[accounts.sustain]` entry, whose account's main purse becomes the sustain purse ([v2.2.0 notes](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0)).
- In `sustain` mode the auction mints `ratio × total rewards` into the sustain purse and multiplies each validator's reward by `1 − ratio` ([auction.rs](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L696-L711), [detail.rs](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction/detail.rs#L1658-L1659)). The purse is recorded in the Mint's named keys as [`sustain_purse`](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/system/mint/constants.rs#L47-L48).
- Mainnet uses `ratio = [2,8]` and purse `uref-5bd5c35f2897844b307b9efbb2d7f01f4d51a2ecea8d8b88abd4e3c3a4594693-007`, which the file's comment assigns to a "CA Ecosystem Sustainability account" ([mainnet](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L182-L184)). Why it matters: 2/8 (25%) of distributed rewards go to that purse, so validators and delegators share the other 6/8.
- Mainnet's 2.2.0 chainspec already has this `sustain` setting, with the same ratio and purse ([a9e0a1d](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/a9e0a1d3be079ca40ec1d852d528a555941cadc5/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L182-L184)). The mainnet file at the v2.2.0 tag reads `standard`, but that file is the pre-release configuration described above ([v2.2.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.0/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L182)); `sustain` first appears in a tagged mainnet file at [v2.2.1](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.1/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L184).
- One-time issuance (CVV008): the v2.2.0 notes say new tokens "equivalent to maximally 33% of the supply at the time of the upgrade will be minted to a designated purse as defined by the `global_state.toml`" ([v2.2.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0)). The mainnet `global_state.toml` writes 4,763,091,162,861,000,634 motes to the main purse of "CA Treasury 1", described as "33% of the supply at block 6972606 (14433609569450460644) plus 4942348218 motes - the current balance of this account" ([global_state.toml](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/global_state.toml#L1-L11)). One CSPR is 1,000,000,000 motes ([docs](https://docs.casper.network/concepts/glossary/M), [source](https://github.com/casper-network/docs-redux/blob/c78e3c2040d30fd8c42cb95bc8a3b308db9d1a56/versioned_docs/version-2.0.0/concepts/glossary/M.md#L19)), so the written balance is about 4.76 billion CSPR.
- The v2.2.0 notes tie that file to the 2.2.0 upgrade ([v2.2.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0)). Commit a9e0a1d added it with the 2.2.0 mainnet settings, and ebbdf71, the v2.2.1 release-config commit, deleted it ([a9e0a1d](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/commit/a9e0a1d3be079ca40ec1d852d528a555941cadc5), [ebbdf71](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/commit/ebbdf719bee6c34345a50a2387b9d36f70a81262)). It is absent from the [v2.2.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/tree/v2.2.0/resources/mainnet) and [v2.2.1](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/tree/v2.2.1/resources/mainnet) tags, and present at [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/tree/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet) because v2.2.2 does not contain ebbdf71 ([compare v2.2.2...v2.2.1](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/compare/v2.2.2...v2.2.1)). Why it matters: the file at v2.2.2 is the 2.2.0 issuance file, not one written for 2.2.2.
- Every protocol upgrade from v2.2.0 recalculates total supply by summing balance records and, if the sum differs from the Mint's figure, replaces it and logs a WARN; the notes add that the WARN "will always be emitted if there is a modification to balance records during a protocol upgrade with the `global_state.toml`" ([v2.2.0 notes](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0), [call](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/protocol_upgrade.rs#L221), [rewrite](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/protocol_upgrade.rs#L1643-L1653)). v2.2.0 also fixed a 2.0 genesis bug that "did not include delegator stakes towards the total supply" ([storage CHANGELOG](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/CHANGELOG.md#L26-L29)).
- Testnet economics differ: the testnet file at v2.2.2 says "Annual issuance: 0.25%" with `round_seigniorage_rate = [1, 1000000000]`, as it has since [v2.1.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.1.2/resources/testnet/chainspec.toml#L58-L66), and sends its 2/8 sustain share to a purse the file assigns to the "faucet account for testnet" ([issuance](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/testnet/chainspec.toml#L58-L66), [sustain](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/testnet/chainspec.toml#L182-L185)). Why it matters: testnet reward and issuance figures do not predict mainnet ones.

## Staking rules (validators and delegators)

- `minimum_bid_amount` fell from 10,000 CSPR to 500 CSPR (10_000_000_000_000 to 500_000_000_000 motes) ([v2.0.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.0/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L75-L77), [v2.2.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L75-L77)). The production file changed at [v2.1.2](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.1.2/resources/production/chainspec.toml#L77); the v2.2.0 notes say mainnet got it with 2.2.0, "matching the minimum delegation amount for delegators" ([v2.2.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0)).
- Since v2.1.2 (on mainnet since 2.2.0), `withdraw_bid` for more than the staked amount fails again with `UnbondTooLarge`, as in 1.x; in 2.0 it unbonded "up to amount" ([code](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L240-L247), [v2.1.2 notes](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.1.2)). Withdrawing to a stake below `minimum_bid_amount` still fully unbonds the validator and all its delegators ([code](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L255-L285)). Since v2.2.2 a zero-amount `withdraw_bid` fails with `BondTooSmall` ([code](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L229-L233), [commit](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/commit/6e03c5a942da6e88e4306638338ab40af7c955ff)).
- Delegation limits in v2.1.1: the notes say the binary "corrected issues with minimum_delegation_amount applying due to logic error" ([v2.1.1](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.1.1)), while node/CHANGELOG.md says it fixed "incorrect setting of maximum delegator amount on validator bids" ([node CHANGELOG](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/node/CHANGELOG.md#L19-L22)). The code change covers both limits ([37fe0b4](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/commit/37fe0b47f580236840a722a71086c4be977b9966), [0679f48](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/commit/0679f48b660cef7605d520990dcfd0ecf3e528e3)). From v2.0.1 through v2.1.0, the `add_bid` path for an existing bid skipped storing new limits unless they raised the minimum or lowered the maximum ([v2.0.1](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.0.1/storage/src/system/auction/detail.rs#L1364-L1381), [v2.1.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.1.0/storage/src/system/auction/detail.rs#L1364-L1381)). Commit [37fe0b4](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/commit/37fe0b47f580236840a722a71086c4be977b9966) "Reorder early exit for delegator limits" stores them first ([v2.2.2 detail.rs](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction/detail.rs#L1396-L1408)), and commit [0679f48](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/commit/0679f48b660cef7605d520990dcfd0ecf3e528e3) "Fix override of delegator limits" makes both limits optional, so an existing bid keeps its limits when they are omitted ([auction.rs](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L136-L139)). Why it matters: from v2.0.1 until v2.1.1, a change that only lowered a validator's minimum or raised its maximum was not stored.
- Delegation limits in v2.1.2: the check uses a delegator's existing stake plus the new amount; when that fix activates, delegators below the validator's minimum are unbonded and those above the maximum are partially unbonded down to it ([v2.2.0 notes](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0)).
- `minimum_delegation_rate` (v2.2.0, CVV006) is the lowest delegation rate a validator may set, 0-100; it is 0 on mainnet, which the notes say "keeps behavior inert" until a future vote ([mainnet](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L88-L89), [v2.2.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0)). `add_bid` and reservations with a lower rate fail with `DelegationRateTooSmall` (auction error 64) ([add_bid](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L95-L97), [reservations](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction/detail.rs#L939-L941), [error](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/system/auction/error.rs#L416-L421)). Existing bids below the minimum are not changed by the upgrade ([v2.2.0](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/releases/tag/v2.2.0)); the value is stored in the Auction's named key [`minimum_delegation_rate`](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/types/src/system/auction/constants.rs#L123-L124).
- The v2.2.0 notes also say a change of stake through `withdraw_bid` will require the minimum rate, but at v2.2.2 `withdraw_bid` takes no delegation rate and checks none ([code](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/storage/src/system/auction.rs#L217-L222)).

The mainnet file at v2.2.2 instead has a 75/100 `refund` ratio, `burn` fee handling and `payment_limited` pricing ([refund](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L117), [fees](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L126), [pricing](https://github.com/casper-network/casper-node/blob/v2.2.2/resources/mainnet/chainspec.toml#L135)).
